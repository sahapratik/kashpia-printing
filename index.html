01 — Company

We turn materials into meaningful packaging.

2007

Incorporated in 2007, Kashpia Printing and Packaging Ind. (KPPI) manufactures and supplies corrugated cartons, mono cartons, self-adhesive labels, labels, foil prints and holographic labels.

The company is backed by an infrastructure facility equipped with advanced machines and technologies, with the facility divided into units for swift execution of every process.
